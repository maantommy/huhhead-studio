Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

HuhHead Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the HuhHead YouTube channel.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, through Google's YouTube Data API, it accesses only the HuhHead channel: it uploads and schedules the channel's own videos, reads and replies to comments on those videos, and reads the channel's view and subscriber statistics.

What it stores

The sign-in token and the channel's statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Public comment text is read to write replies and is kept with the reply log on that computer. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, and no data is used for advertising.

Other people

HuhHead Studio does not collect personal information from viewers or anyone else. Only public comments on HuhHead's own videos are read, in order to reply to them.

Google API Services

HuhHead Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the HuhHead channel page.
